- Logo: aset identitas Axura yang diberikan dalam pekerjaan sebelumnya; dipertahankan, tanpa menggambar ulang simbol. File: `public/assets/brand-logo.jpg`.

## Visual utama

- File produksi: `public/assets/axura-orbit.webp`.
- Dibuat menggunakan alat **imagegen bawaan**, kemudian dikonversi ke WebP untuk pemuatan website.
- Visual ini adalah ilustrasi brand, bukan gambar produk fisik perusahaan.
- Prompt akhir:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium technology company website hero artwork for Audev Axura Technology, whose established brand color is deep violet. Create one exquisitely rendered abstract 3D sculpture: two thick intertwined elliptical orbital ribbons, one polished dark purple anodized metal and one translucent optical glass with violet refraction, ascending around a small brilliant white core. Elegant physical structure, organic precise geometry, beautifully lit material, a few tiny restrained chrome spheres. Wide 3:2 composition, main sculpture occupies the central-right two thirds, generous dark negative space on left. Background perfectly near-black plum #110D1D, soft smoky violet floor glow but no environment, no landscape. High-end Octane/Cinema4D product render, refined and minimal art direction, sharp details and tactile reflections, dramatic studio rim light from upper right. Purple, amethyst, pale lavender, chrome accents. Intended as a premium digital engineering studio brand hero, mature corporate editorial aesthetic, no cyberpunk. Absolutely no text, letters, logos, interface elements, numbers, watermark, grids or badges. Deliver a finished high-resolution landscape artwork.
